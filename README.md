# Raising a Child — Personal Preparation Notes

A small collection of personal planning documents for a Chinese-Indonesian couple in Singapore expecting their first child — a boy — in about **11 weeks** (wife is in week 29). The family will be renting a **689 sqft, partially furnished HDB flat**, and wants to prepare without cluttering a small home or losing sight of what actually matters.

## Contents

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [`baby-preparation-shopping-list.md`](baby-preparation-shopping-list.md) | Everything to buy (and what to skip) for the first baby boy in a 689 sqft HDB. Every item is scored 0–10 for importance, with a **Buy / Buy later / Borrow / Skip** verdict, indicative prices in SGD, a timeline game plan, and Singapore-specific tips (HDB space rules, Baby Bonus/CDA, safe-sleep guidance, etc.). |
| [`parenting-guide-raising-a-healthy-grounded-boy.md`](parenting-guide-raising-a-healthy-grounded-boy.md) | A long-form parenting guide addressing seven family concerns — from health, picky eating, and honesty to critical thinking, cooking skills, responsibility, sexuality, and sexual orientation — with an honest, evidence-informed "inputs over outcomes" philosophy, an age-by-age action plan, a family charter, and Singapore-specific resources. |

## How to use these documents

- **Read the shopping list first** if the baby is arriving soon — it is organized by priority and timeline (now → week 33, weeks 34–36, weeks 37–40, and the first months home).
- **Read the parenting guide slowly**, section by section. It starts with a reality check and a foundation (secure attachment), then works through each concern, and ends with practical tools, an age-by-age plan, and a 15-point family charter you can adopt.
- Both documents are living notes: update them as plans, budgets, and the family's needs change.
