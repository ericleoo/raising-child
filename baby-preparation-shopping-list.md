# Baby Preparation Shopping List — For a First Baby Boy in a 689 sqft HDB

**Who this is for:** a Chinese-Indonesian couple in Singapore, expecting their first child (boy) in about **11 weeks** (wife is in week 29). The family is about to rent a **689 sqft, partially furnished HDB flat** — intentionally under-furnished so it doesn't feel cramped.

**What this document does:** lists everything to buy (and what to skip), each item scored **0–10** for importance, with a clear **Buy / Buy later / Borrow / Skip** verdict, indicative prices in SGD, and Singapore-specific notes.

> ⚠️ **Reality check first:** you do **not** need most of the stuff in a typical "baby registry." A newborn needs, for the first weeks: a safe place to sleep, milk (and the gear to feed it), diapers, clothes, and a way to leave the house. Everything else can wait until you've met your baby and know your routine. In a small flat, **space is your scarcest resource — buy only for the next 2–3 months at a time.**

## 3. Sleep (the #1 priority)

> Singapore guidance (Ministry of Health): babies should sleep **alone, on their back, in a crib** with a firm mattress and **no loose bedding, pillows, bumpers, or soft toys** — in the **parents' room for at least the first 6 months** (room-sharing is recommended for up to 12 months). This directly shapes what you buy: in a 689 sqft flat, a cot in your room beats a separate nursery.

## 10. Money: what it costs & what Singapore pays you

**Rough first-year spend (buying smart, mid-range):** big-ticket gear ~$1,000–1,800 (cot, stroller, car seat, pump, monitor), consumables ~$80–120/month (diapers, wipes, formula if used), services (confinement, insurance, PD visits) ~$2,000–6,000 depending on choices.

**What Singapore gives back (Baby Bonus, as of 2025 — SG60 enhancements):**
- **Cash gift:** **$11,000** for your first child, paid in 5 instalments over 18 months.
- **CDA (Child Development Account) First Step Grant:** **$5,000** — opens when you open a CDA bank account (DBS/POSB, OCBC, UOB, Standard Chartered) — do this right after birth.
- **CDA government matching:** dollar-for-dollar, up to **$6,000** per child for your first child (cap increases with more children). Money goes into your CDA and is usable for childcare, healthcare, and some therapy/equipment at approved providers.
- Plus: **Medisave Maternity Package** (hospital delivery co-payment), **paternity leave 4 weeks** + **shared parental leave** for mum's employer, and **GST vouchers/CDC** rounds you may be eligible for.

**Practical tip:** put your Baby Bonus into a high-interest savings account or an investment for the child later — the CDA matching alone is a guaranteed ~6–11% instant return depending on how much you contribute, so **maximise the CDA match before any other "baby investment."**

## 11. Space-saving rules for 689 sqft

1. **Room-share** — baby sleeps in your bedroom for 6–12 months. No nursery needed.
2. **Buy multi-purpose:** muslins do 5 jobs; a backpack is the diaper bag; a floor mat is the changing table.
3. **Collapsible > fixed:** collapsible bathtub, foldable stroller, stackable bins.
4. **Buy for the next 2–3 months only** — no 0–9 m clothing stockpiles, no high chair in month 1.
5. **Second-hand is your friend:** Carousell and SG parent Telegram groups for strollers, bouncers, clothes, and toys. **Always buy new: mattress, car seat (crash history unknown), and anything electric with a safety recall.**
6. **Declutter as you go** — sell/give away outgrown items on Carousell; a small flat punishes hoarding.
7. **Storage trick:** under-bed boxes + ceiling-high wall shelves + door-mounted organisers turn dead space into a baby wardrobe.

## 12. Do-not-buy list (quick reference)

- ❌ **Baby walker** — banned for sale in Singapore.
- ❌ **Bumper pads, pillows, loose blankets, cot bumpers** — SIDS risk.
- ❌ **Sterilised baby water** — tap water is safe.
- ❌ **Cord blood banking** (unless medically advised).
- ❌ **Baby food maker / full weaning kit** — not needed until 6 months.
- ❌ **Multiple sizes of clothes up front** — buy as baby grows.
- ❌ **NB diapers in bulk** — baby outgrows them in ~3–4 weeks.
- ❌ **Dedicated nursery furniture** — you don't have the space or the need.
- ❌ **Overnight-monitor socks / wearables** — anxiety machines.
